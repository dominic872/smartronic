10 0 * * * /opt/alt/php82/usr/bin/php /home/u398852039/domains/smartronic.online/public_html/admin_v2/smart/snapshot_pricing_data.php

What it does:
- Runs daily at 12:10 AM.
- Creates data_snapshots/data_YYYY-MM.json for the current month if it does not already exist.
- Does not overwrite an existing month snapshot, so older profit calculations remain stable.
