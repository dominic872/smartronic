Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to commonly asked questions about our products and services.

Do you offer FREE CCTV installation services?

Yes, our certified technicians ensure professional installation with optimal camera placement and seamless system functionality, all included at no additional cost.

Is there a warranty on the CCTV systems?

All our CCTV systems come with a manufacturer’s warranty, and we also offer extended service packages for added peace of mind.

Do you provide Annual Maintenance Services?

Yes, we offer comprehensive Annual Maintenance Services, including regular system checks and prompt repairs, to keep your CCTV system running smoothly.

How long does the installation process take?

Most installations are completed within a single day, although the exact timeline depends on the project’s scope and site conditions.

How do I get started with purchasing the CCTV?

Simply fill out the get quotation form or call us to schedule a free consultation. Our experts will assess your security needs and recommend the best solution for you.

What is included in the annual maintenance plan?

Our annual maintenance plan covers routine system checks, necessary repairs, and lifetime free replacements for defective cameras.

What about CCTV privacy concerns?

We prioritize your privacy and ensure that all installations comply with local privacy laws. Additionally, our systems include features like access control, password protection, and encryption to safeguard your data and footage.

Can I monitor the CCTV footage remotely?

Absolutely! Our systems support remote monitoring via smartphone or computer, allowing you to view live footage from anywhere at any time.

What is the difference between DVR and NVR?

DVR is best suited for analog cameras, while NVR is designed for IP cameras. If you’re unsure, our team will guide you to the right choice based on your requirements.

How do I decide the number of cameras I need?

Our experts will evaluate your property and recommend the ideal number of cameras to ensure complete coverage and optimal security.

What happens if a camera stops working?

Under our annual maintenance plan, defective cameras are replaced for free, ensuring uninterrupted protection.

Do your cameras work at night?

Yes, all our cameras are equipped with automatic night vision, delivering clear and reliable footage even in complete darkness.
